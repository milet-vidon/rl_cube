# V10 eleven-history development candidate

V10 changes only the reviewed data protocol. It uses the frozen V9 BASE/HOLD/UPDATE policy, named previous-command arm and recovery-finger anchors, unchanged arm projection cap of 0.025 rad, original action projection, loss, public phase gate and recurrent actor. It does not incorporate V11 candidates or native-articulation data.

Eleven same-seed, same-physics development histories contain 13,051 eligible transitions: the original nine histories (9,523 frames) and 1,725 plus 1,803 fully completed transitions from two failed V8 episodes. Their final teacher queries raised before returning; those two queries have no fabricated after-state, actor/action or label. Original failure and ineligibility flags remain unchanged. The exception-prefix consumer and its completed proofs are separately reviewed; old V9 admission remains frozen. Raw historical datasets and pretrained checkpoints are referenced by their pinned manifests and hashes; this candidate archive does not duplicate their large artifacts.

All original continuous labels remain. Frozen V9 execution-branch coverage reports zero interval gaps, zero missing float32 witnesses and no genuine correction hidden by the original public mask. These are representation checks, not neural fit, execution safety or successful-episode proof. Existing seven step499 BASE, two step545 ring HOLD and one step560 index HOLD regressions are unchanged; the new histories are not relabelled to satisfy them.

Hidden64, hidden128 and hidden256 preflights use the same qualification. CPU/CUDA branch and classification definitions match bitwise. Archived root authorizations and fixed launch observations cover three 5000-epoch configurations: hidden128/seed20261104, hidden256/seed20261104 and hidden128/seed20261105. Root started these development training queues after their preflights. The fixed observations record launch state only and do not imply training completion. The candidate qualification itself retains training_authorized=false. Training completion products, changing training journals, models and optimizer state are deliberately outside this candidate archive; it makes no physical-success claim.

V10 separates its checkpoint/data schema from the unchanged V9 model schema, binds 25 source dependencies and snapshots those dependencies plus the two evaluation modules. Evaluation defaults to preflight; explicit --evaluate is needed to construct a simulator. Original teacher query, right-hand schedule, declared gain event, safety and wrist gates, termination, stepping and cleanup remain frozen. Checkpoint evidence validation adds no GRU replay; strict actual historical data loading still follows the reviewed readers.

The root installation and supplemental audit-tool binding reports bind fifteen installed files (five production modules, eight tests and two completed-audit tools), verify actual production imports from CODE, and record 63 unique integrated tests with zero failures, errors or skips. The supplemental nine-test rerun uses already counted tests and does not increase the unique count. The evaluation adapter also has a separate independent review. All installed bytes and the original 25-source graph are rechecked during this archive's creation.
