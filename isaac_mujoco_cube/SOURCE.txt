- Uses the upstream fixed core. No articulation root is added.
- Uses upstream total mass convention, 0.0685/27 kg per cubelet plus core.
- Preserves mesh geometry via convex-hull collision; preserves sticker texture.

- Uses PhysX angular damping 0.2/s; does not claim exact MuJoCo frictionloss or armature equivalence.
- Internal/external contact material is frictionless for this geometric-contact bench test.
- External kinematic fixtures provide form closure and normal contact forces.
- Cube poses and velocities are never assigned after physics begins.
- No per-turn joint rewiring or cubelet permutation is performed.

Scope: fixed-core bench test, not a floating cube held by actual robot hands.
